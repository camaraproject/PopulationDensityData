# PopulationDensityData API User Story

| Items | Details |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | The API provides estimation of Population Density for a specific area over a past or future period of time, considering historical anonymized information of the network connected devices in the requested area.|
| **Roles, Actors and Scope** | **Scope:** Retrieve estimated population density for a defined area (polygon or, optionally, a list of geohashes) and time interval; provide insights into population patterns to support strategic planning. <br><br> **Use Case 1 — Drone Route Planning:** Identify zones to avoid due to high population-related risk; determine optimal time window to fly between two points (A–B) minimizing population exposure. <br><br> **Use Case 2 — Urban Planning:** Data-driven optimization of public services/resource allocation; support simulation and impact assessment for urban mobility and infrastructure planning.|
| **Pre-conditions** | The client must provide: <br> - **Requested Area (Required):** Either a polygon defined by coordinates forming a closed loop (`POLYGON`, mandatory support) or a list of geohashes (`GEOHASHLIST`, optional support). <br> - **Start Time (Required):** Start of the requested time interval, which must lie fully in the past or fully in the future (up to 3 months before or after the request time). <br> - **End Time (Required):** End of the requested time interval; the difference with Start Time must not exceed 7 days. <br> - **Precision (Optional):** Geohash precision level, only for `POLYGON` areas (default 7). It must not be sent for `GEOHASHLIST`, where each geohash defines its own precision. <br> - **Sink (Optional):** Destination system/endpoint for delivery. <br> - **Sink Credentials (Optional):** AuthN/AuthZ details to access the sink.|
| **Activities / Steps** | 1) The client submits a request including the inputs above. <br> 2) The API responds **synchronously** (response body) or **asynchronously** (delivered to sink), including: <br> - **a) Cell Coordinates:** Geohash string representing the cell. <br> - **b) Population Density Metrics:** per cell in **1-hour intervals** with **min / avg / max** population. <br> - **c) Data Type (k-anonymity):** `LOW_DENSITY` (below threshold, cannot disclose), `DENSITY_ESTIMATION` (values available and disclosed) or `NO_DATA` (cell outside the operator coverage area). <br> - **d) Status:** indicates whether the requested area is fully supported or whether part of it (`PART_OF_AREA_NOT_SUPPORTED`) or all of it (`AREA_NOT_SUPPORTED`) is outside coverage; `OPERATION_NOT_COMPLETED` is used in asynchronous callbacks when processing fails.|
| **Post-conditions** | The requester can assess population-related information for drone flight operations or urban planning by using estimated or forecasted population density derived from network-connected devices within the requested area.|
| **Exceptions** | - **Invalid input:** missing required fields or incorrect format (e.g., malformed polygon or geohash, missing Start/End Time, invalid precision, `precision` sent with a `GEOHASHLIST`). <br> - **Unsupported values:** parameters outside supported limits (e.g., time interval out of supported range, unsupported precision, `GEOHASHLIST` not supported by the operator). <br> - **Unavailable data:** requested area/time/params not available or cannot be provided due to data protection constraints (e.g., k-anonymity) or because the area is outside the operator coverage.|
